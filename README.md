# Viral Video Hook Formulas

A free, open collection of **hook formulas** for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Each formula has a fill-in-the-blank template and examples from different niches, so you can write a stronger opening line in under a minute.

> A hook is the first one to three seconds of a video. It is the line that decides whether someone keeps watching or scrolls past.

## How to use this list

1. Pick the formula that fits your video.
2. Replace the `[brackets]` in the template with your topic.
3. Say it out loud. If it sounds natural, keep it. If you stumble, shorten it.
4. Test two or three versions and keep the one your audience stays for.

## The formulas

### 1. Curiosity gap
Hint at something the viewer does not know yet.
- Template: `Nobody tells you this about [topic].`
- "Nobody tells you this about starting a podcast."
- "Nobody tells you this about your first month at a new job."
- "Nobody tells you this about buying a used car."

### 2. Bold claim
Make a clear, specific promise you can back up.
- Template: `This one change [result] in [time].`
- "This one change made my edits twice as fast."
- "This one habit fixed my sleep in a week."
- "This one setting doubled my phone's battery life."

### 3. Direct question
Ask what the viewer is already wondering.
- Template: `Why does every [group] [problem]?`
- "Why does every new runner quit in week three?"
- "Why does every small shop make this pricing mistake?"
- "Why does every beginner guitarist stall at the same chord?"

### 4. The mistake
Warn viewers about something costly.
- Template: `Stop doing [habit] if you want [goal].`
- "Stop doing this if you want to save money on groceries."
- "Stop doing this if you want clear skin."
- "Stop doing this if you want your videos to be seen."

### 5. Personal story
Open with a short, honest confession.
- Template: `I wasted [time] before I learned [lesson].`
- "I wasted a year before I learned how to price my work."
- "I wasted months before I learned this editing trick."
- "I wasted my first savings before I learned this."

### 6. Numbers and lists
Give the viewer a clear, finite promise.
- Template: `[Number] [things] every [person] should know.`
- "Three tools every new creator should know."
- "Five questions to ask before you sign a lease."
- "Four mistakes that make your resume get skipped."

### 7. Contrarian
Disagree with common advice, then explain.
- Template: `Everyone says [advice]. Here is why that is wrong.`
- "Everyone says to wake up at five. Here is why that is wrong."
- "Everyone says to post daily. Here is why that is wrong."

### 8. Before and after
Show the change first, then the method.
- Template: `From [before] to [after] in [time]. Here is how.`
- "From zero followers to my first sale in a month. Here is how."
- "From messy to organised in one weekend. Here is how."

### 9. Myth busting
Name a myth and correct it.
- Template: `Myth: [belief]. Truth: [fact].`
- "Myth: you need expensive gear. Truth: your phone is enough."
- "Myth: cardio is the only way to lose weight."

### 10. Time saver
Promise to save effort.
- Template: `Do [task] in [short time] instead of [long time].`
- "Plan your week in five minutes instead of an hour."
- "Clean your inbox in ten minutes, once and for all."

### 11. The challenge
Invite the viewer to try something.
- Template: `Try this for [days] and see what happens.`
- "Try this for seven days and see what happens to your focus."
- "Try this morning routine for a week."

### 12. The secret
Frame useful information as hidden or overlooked.
- Template: `The [thing] most [people] overlook.`
- "The setting most creators overlook on their phone."
- "The clause most renters overlook in a lease."

## What makes a hook work

- **Be specific.** "Save money" is vague. "Cut your grocery bill by a third" is a promise.
- **Keep it short.** Aim for fewer than twelve words.
- **Lead with the payoff or the problem,** not an introduction.
- **Match the video.** A hook that does not deliver loses trust fast.
- **Say it, do not just show it.** Many people watch with sound off, so add the hook as on-screen text too.
