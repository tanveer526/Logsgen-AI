# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this list.

## What we accept
- New hook formulas with a fill-in-the-blank template and at least two examples.
- Better or more varied examples for an existing formula.
- Translations of the templates (Urdu, Arabic, Spanish and others are welcome).
- Fixes to typos and unclear wording.

## What we do not accept
- Promotional links or tools that are not useful on their own.
- Misleading, harmful or deceptive hooks.

## How to contribute
1. Fork the repository.
2. Add your change to `README.md` and keep the existing format.
3. Open a pull request with a short description of what you added and why it works.
